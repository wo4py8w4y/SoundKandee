Complete Studio Engine

Built Like a Visual Modular Synthesizer

Every parameter on every visual layer and transformer is clamped strictly inside user-defined operating bounds, filtered to a custom frequency window, and driven by audio envelopes, Master LFOs, or BPM beat quantization.

AUDIO INGESTION

Triple Capture + Synth Idle Fallback

Switch between Output Mix (Visualizer Session 0), App Audio (Android 10+ MediaProjection PCM-16), and Microphone. When silence exceeds your delay threshold, SyntheticMusicGenerator synthesizes multi-voice music through the real FFT pipeline and yields instantaneously when live audio returns.

MODULATION MATRIX

Bounded ScopedParam & Bandpass Windows

Every parameter defines [scopeMin..scopeMax] bounds inside hard hardware limits, 22 drivers, 5 response curves (Linear, Exp, Log, Quantize, Gate), attack/release slew, inversion, lock pins, and per-layer 20 Hz–20 kHz bandpass filter windows.

TEMPO & LFO ENGINE

Auto-Beat PLL, Tap-Tempo & 3 LFO Buses

Spectral-flux beat detection with 65–180 BPM octave folding and PLL phase tracking, paired with instant tap-tempo downbeat snapping (÷2 / 1x / ×2). Includes 3 Global Master LFO Buses with oscilloscope visualization and beat-grid FX step quantization.

MILKDROP + AGSL GPU

12 Generators & 17 Overlappable FX

Stack infinite blend-mode layers (*Screen, Add, Difference, Exclusion, Overlay, Multiply*) alongside offscreen ping-pong Feedback Echo Trails and fused Android 13+ AGSL shaders for MilkDrop per-pixel warps, zoomexp tunnels, pinwheels, solarize, and CRT curves.

PHYSICAL GESTURE

Tap-the-Device Randomizer

Uses Sensor.TYPE_LINEAR_ACCELERATION (with gravity-filtered TYPE_ACCELEROMETER fallback and zero permissions) so physically tapping your phone randomizes options, mutates within your scopes, or switches presets—preserving locked layers & palettes.

LOCAL PRESET STUDIO

Complete Snapshot Management

Ships with 8 built-in presets and 12 color palettes plus a 2–5 stop custom color editor. Save, overwrite, duplicate, rename, delete, or revert full configurations in Jetpack DataStore—and swipe into distraction-free edge-to-edge Zen Mode.
